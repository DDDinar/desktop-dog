Use case: identity-preserve, DSLR natural photograph. Create one transparent production game sprite atlas, FOUR columns by THREE rows, precisely twelve panels, 4:3 landscape. Each panel is a photo cutout of the SAME real chestnut dog and the SAME charcoal grey oval fabric pet bed shown in input 1 and input 2. The third and fourth photographs show the dog's exact face and coat. Faithfully copy its darker ears, wide warm-brown muzzle, light tan nose bridge, glossy dark eyes, black matte nose and real fine chestnut curls with a little natural unevenness, not sculpted fur. Include the complete bed and dog in every cutout, no crops. All panels have exactly the same fixed camera, the same exact bed pose/location, the same dog proportions, scale and lighting. Camera 22 degrees downward, view looking at the front of the dog's face and the long broad side of the bed. Bed is an elongated oval across screen horizontally with soft raised sides like references, not a round basket; dog's head near LEFT center, tail visible near the right. Bed width, silhouette and position must be perfectly fixed to the pixels of each equal grid cell, even when the dog stands in it. The dog's eyes look toward the viewer or slightly left. Restrict change to the pose. Space the cells evenly and provide generous transparent gutters. Read order left to right:
Row 1: (1) relaxed lying, muzzle raised, looking at viewer, forelegs resting across the edge as in reference, (2) same pose, slow blink, (3) lying with nose lowered onto the edge and eyes half closed, (4) sleeping head on edge, eyes closed.
Row 2: (1) just lifting its chest from resting, forelegs on the edge, (2) rising onto forelegs, hips still down, (3) seated upright inside the bed, (4) standing naturally on four paws inside the bed, its body diagonal and tail at rest on screen right, both eyes visible to viewer.
Row 3: (1) same standing body, tail raised swishing right toward the camera, (2) same standing body, tail swung left behind the back, (3) same standing body, nose just lowering toward bed rim to smell it, (4) same standing body, sniffing the rim.
True alpha transparency, no text, no room, no floor, no visible grid, no cast shadow outside the bed. Real photography, no 3D renders, cartoon, collar, harness or gloss.
